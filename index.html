One launch per mission. Points: 100 for a perfect flight (within 0.1 km/s). You lose 3 points for every 0.1 km/s you are off. A crash scores 0. Everyone on the same challenge set gets the same targets, so you can compare scores.

Simplified model: only the planet's gravity acts on the spacecraft during the flyby, speeds are measured compared to the Sun, and the planet's own motion is not visibly changed. In real flybys the planet slows by an amount far too tiny to measure.
